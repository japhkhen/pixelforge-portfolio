About me

A software engineer who sweats the quiet details.

PixelForge is the name on the work: interfaces people understand, and systems that keep their promises.

Kumasi, Ghana

Hello. I’m PixelForge.

I like products that feel quiet in the hand: obvious buttons, fast pages, and screens that still look cared for the tenth time you open them. I work with founders, clinics, shops, and studios that want their digital home to feel as considered as the business behind it.

The work covers the words on the screen, the layout, the motion, and the code underneath. A marketing site, a booking tool, a dashboard, a checkout — each one gets the same standard: beautiful, clear, and built to last.

If you are in Kumasi, anywhere in Ghana, or building from far away, we can start with a conversation.
